# Demo scenario

The demo walks one allowance procurement case through the whole chain, using synthetic data modelled on a large Korean steelmaker's footprint (Korea, Japan and Singapore plants, assumed totals of 70 / 40 / 10 million tonnes).

1. **Control room**: plant map, live minute cards (data arrives every minute), allowance price tile with a stock-style chart (monthly / weekly / daily).
2. **Calculation**: Scope 1 / Scope 2 results, abatement-cost curves, report preview.
3. **Procurement and approval**: pick a case in the table. The one-box detail shows the requester's reason, application and job identifiers, the history and the decision area. The AI panel on the right answers questions about the selected case using only computed facts.
4. **Decision**: an authorized reviewer approves or rejects with MFA. Nothing executes without it.
5. **Simulated execution and evidence**: the permit is verified and claimed, the simulator dispatches, evidence is signed, and the audit view shows the chain.
6. **Knowledge search**: law (Korea, Japan, Singapore) and 5-year history retrieval with source citations.
7. **Topology**: the resource map, with load-balancer-to-backend links and path finding.
