# Limitations and open decisions

- Demonstration only: synthetic data, simulated execution, no market connectivity.
- Singapore regulation text is incomplete; a legal source PDF is still needed.
- Open decisions: customer identity provider for the footprint service, outbound scope of the export boundary, direction of the agent edit screen.
- The cost service currently models energy cost only.
- Measured in a headless browser only: Korean font rendering and mobile layouts were not verified on real devices.
- Instrumentation covers Korean services; Japan and Southeast Asia are intentionally excluded for data residency.
- No license has been chosen.
